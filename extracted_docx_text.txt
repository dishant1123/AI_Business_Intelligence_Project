1. Project Overview
Problem Statement
Develop an AI-powered Business Operations and Decision Intelligence Platform that uses multiple autonomous AI agents to analyze business data, identify operational problems, generate actionable recommendations, and assist management in making data-driven decisions.
The system should allow business owners or managers to interact with an intelligent AI platform through a React.js frontend.
The backend must be developed using Python FastAPI, while CrewAI will orchestrate multiple specialized AI agents powered by Large Language Models (LLMs).
The agents should collaborate with each other, exchange information, analyze business scenarios, validate results, and generate comprehensive business intelligence reports.
The objective is to develop a fully functional industry-level multi-agent application rather than a basic chatbot or standalone AI tool.

3. Multi-Agent Architecture
The application must implement at least six specialized AI agents using CrewAI.
Agent 1: Data Analyst Agent
Responsibilities:
Analyze business datasets.
Identify trends and patterns.
Calculate important business KPIs.
Identify unusual changes in business performance.
Generate analytical summaries.
Agent 2: Marketing Agent
Responsibilities:
Analyze customer behavior.
Identify customer segments.
Evaluate marketing performance.
Identify potential growth opportunities.
Recommend customer retention strategies.
Agent 3: Finance Agent
Responsibilities:
Analyze revenue and expenses.
Calculate profit margins.
Identify financial risks.
Evaluate monthly financial performance.
Recommend cost optimization strategies.
Agent 4: Operations Agent
Responsibilities:
Identify operational inefficiencies.
Analyze resource utilization.
Detect operational bottlenecks.
Evaluate productivity.
Recommend process improvements.
Agent 5: Strategy Agent
Responsibilities:
Receive outputs from other agents.
Combine business insights.
Identify business growth opportunities.
Prepare strategic recommendations.
Generate an actionable business improvement plan.
Agent 6: Verification Agent
Responsibilities:
Validate findings generated by other agents.
Cross-check numerical calculations against source data.
Identify unsupported statements.
Detect contradictory recommendations.
Flag low-confidence findings.
Approve or reject the final report based on verification results.

7. Mandatory Agent Workflow
Business Scenario: A company uploads its last six months of sales, customer, finance and operational data.
The following workflow must be implemented:
Step 1 – Data Analyst Agent
Analyzes uploaded datasets.
Calculates business KPIs.
Identifies monthly trends and anomalies.
Step 2 – Marketing Agent
Receives relevant business data and analytical findings.
Analyzes customer segments and marketing opportunities.
Step 3 – Finance Agent
Analyzes revenue, expenses and profitability.
Identifies financial risks and cost optimization opportunities.
Step 4 – Operations Agent
Analyzes operational efficiency.
Identifies bottlenecks and performance issues.
Step 5 – Strategy Agent
Receives outputs from all specialized agents.
Prepares a consolidated business strategy.
Generates actionable recommendations.
Step 6 – Verification Agent
Validates the consolidated findings.
Checks calculations and supporting evidence.
Flags unsupported or inconsistent recommendations.
Sends the report back for correction if necessary.
Final Output: The verified Business Intelligence Report must be displayed on the React.js dashboard and saved in the database.
8. Additional Advanced Requirements
The following requirements are compulsory:
All agents must communicate and share their outputs with other agents.
Implement Sequential and/or Hierarchical CrewAI execution wherever appropriate.
Develop FastAPI endpoints independently instead of keeping all logic inside the CrewAI implementation.
React.js must consume the independently developed FastAPI endpoints.
Implement proper API validation and exception handling.
Maintain agent-wise execution logs.
Store previous analysis reports in the database.
Implement at least one Human-in-the-Loop approval mechanism before executing a consequential business action.
Include a verification mechanism to reduce LLM hallucinations.
Use actual dataset values for numerical analysis instead of allowing the LLM to invent business metrics.
Display the execution status of individual agents on the frontend.
Implement proper authentication and role-based access control.
9. Human-in-the-Loop Implementation
The system must include at least one approval workflow.
Example: The Strategy Agent recommends increasing the marketing budget by 15%.
Strategy Agent generates the recommendation.
Verification Agent validates the recommendation.
System sends the recommendation to the business manager.
Manager reviews the recommendation.
Manager approves or rejects the action.
The system records the decision and approval history.
The system must not execute consequential business actions automatically without authorized approval.
